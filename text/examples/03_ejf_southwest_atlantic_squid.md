# EJF: Southwest Atlantic Squid Fishery — Human Rights & Ecological Crisis

**Source:** https://ejfoundation.org/news-media/new-investigation-exposes-human-rights-abuses-and-ecological-crisis-in-the-southwest-atlantic-squid-fishery
**Published:** September 2025
**Type:** Regional investigation + film

## Summary

Hundreds of distant-water vessels are plundering Argentine shortfin squid populations just beyond Argentina's EEZ boundary — an area known as **"Mile 201"**. The fleet is so vast **its lights can be seen from space**.

## Key Statistics

- Between 2019 and 2024, fishing hours by **Chinese squid vessels in the region increased by 85%**, even as landings showed alarming decline
- Fishing pressure in the unregulated high-seas fishery is **over 4x greater** than within adjacent regulated Argentine waters
- Argentine shortfin squid provided **12.2% of the global squid catch** in 2023
- Almost **two-thirds of Chinese squid vessels** were linked to deaths or physical assaults on board
- Crew reported illegal and cruel targeting of wildlife including **shark finning** and deliberate killing of marine mammals
