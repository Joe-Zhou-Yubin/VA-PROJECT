## What It Is

Every 2 years, NOAA Fisheries identifies nations whose vessels have engaged in IUU fishing, forced labor, bycatch of protected species, or shark catch without adequate regulations. Identified nations enter a 2-year consultation period. Failure to address issues can result in:
- Denial of US port access for fishing vessels
- Import restrictions on fish products

## 2026 Report Key Findings

- **6 nations identified for IUU fishing**
- **2 nations identified for forced labor** in fishing
- Since 2009, NOAA has made a total of **78 unique identifications**

## Prior Report (2023) Identified Nations

- **IUU fishing:** China, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Mexico, Russia, Senegal, Taiwan
- **Forced labor:** China, Taiwan
- Mexico was **negatively certified** for continued IUU (lanchas in US waters) — Mexican fishing vessels denied US port entry

## Operation North Pacific Guard (2026)

NOAA enforcement officers deployed aboard Canadian Coast Guard vessel, patrolled 8,000+ nautical miles, conducted dozens of boardings. Documented violations including:
- Shark finning
- Possession of endangered species
- Failure to accurately report catch

Partners: DFO Canada, Canadian Coast Guard, RCMP, USCG, Japan Fisheries Agency, Korea Coast Guard.
